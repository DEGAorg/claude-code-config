## About DEGA

DEGA is building tools and infrastructure to empower developers to launch AI-powered prediction market automation. With a strong focus on developer experience and community-driven innovation, DEGA simplifies prediction market development through Canon — a CLI toolkit that lets developers bootstrap, configure, and deploy trading strategies from the terminal in minutes.

## Core Technology

- **Canon CLI**: `canon init` scaffolds your project; `canon start` launches your automation. One command to go from idea to running bot.
- **TypeScript Strategy Templates**: Pre-built code base with typed interfaces for market data fetching, strategy logic, and execution — ready to customize.
- **Automation System View**: Live flow diagrams showing each step of your automation (data fetch → analysis → decision → execution), real-time logs, and status monitoring.

## Prize Pool

Participants are ranked by the profit % their automation generates during the competition period. Top performer takes the prize.

## Themes

Build AI-powered automations focused on prediction market microstructures:

- **Arbitrage Detection**: Exploit price discrepancies for the same event across different markets or within the same platform.
- **Cross-Market Analysis**: Correlate positions across related markets — when one moves, capitalize on the lag in another.
- **Speed-Based Opportunity**: Act on publicly available statistical data (team records, matchups, injury reports) before market prices adjust.

Canon's built-in templates focus on these microstructure-based approaches. Participants are free to explore other strategy types, but Canon does not provide templates for news sentiment or opinion-based strategies.

## Submission Requirements

Each submission must include:

- **Project Description:** Strategy approach and intended automation behavior.
- **Source Code:** Publicly accessible on GitHub.
- **Documentation:** Setup instructions and an `about` section describing the automation's flow.
